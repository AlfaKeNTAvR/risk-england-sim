# Printing the table pieces

Source: the "Medieval England Table Pieces" design canvas (`docs/print/`). Export each canvas page as a PDF and print from the PDF.

Printer assumed: **HP ENVY 6455e** (owner said "HP NV 450"; confirm the model). It duplexes automatically up to 180 g/m² (HP Brochure / 48 lb cardstock setting). Heavier paper (up to 300 g/m² photo) only prints one side per pass: print the front, put the sheet back in turned over, print the back.

## Settings for every sheet

- Paper size: Letter (8.5 x 11 in).
- Scale: 100% / Actual size. Never "Fit to page": it shrinks the pieces and moves the backs out of line.
- Color, Best quality.
- Paper type: matches the paper loaded (Plain for test runs, HP Brochure / cardstock for real pieces).
- Test print each job on plain paper first and hold the sheet up to a light to check the backs line up.

## Jobs

| Canvas page | Sheets | Sides | Duplex flip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Century event cards | 4 | one side | — |
| Century plaques | 2 | both | long edge (portrait) |
| Era board | 1 | both | short edge (landscape) |
| Diplomacy: seals (A) or treaty cards (B) | 1 | one side | — |
| House rules, letter | 1 | both | long edge |
| House rules, A5 book size | 2 | both | long edge |
| Where it goes | 1 | one side, plain paper | — |

PDF page order inside each double-sided job is front, back, front, back.

## Why the backs line up

- Plaques: full-width, centered pieces in the same three row positions on front and back. A long-edge flip mirrors left-right only, so the rows stay put. The printer still drifts a few mm (first test print: the long edge moved), so only the FRONT has the rounded cut shape; the BACK is one square parchment block that runs 5 mm past every piece. Cut from the front side. The three plaques on a sheet touch with no gap and have square corners: one straight cut at the small margin ticks separates two plaques, then round every corner with the punch.
- Era board: symmetric margins on a landscape sheet; a short-edge flip keeps it upright.
- A5 rules: each A5 page is centered on letter, with 5 mm of extra parchment all round on both sides. Trim marks are on the fronts only (pages 1 and 3): cut from the front, 148 x 210 mm, the rulebook's page size.

## Finishing

- No outline strokes: cut along the edge of the parchment colour (4 mm gap between pieces), round corners with the 5 mm punch. Every piece keeps 5 mm clear inside its edge.
- No laminating or dry-erase marker needed: pact turns, the opener and the current event are all tracked with cavalry pieces.
- Plaques: V/VI, VII/VIII, IX/X, XI/XII, XIII/XIV, how-to/blank.
