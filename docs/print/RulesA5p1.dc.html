Risk: The Battle for Medieval England

Our House Rules

Everything not here follows the rulebook.

Your turn

1

Place troops. 3, plus the territory chart, regions, missions and card trades. You may flip a power card.

2

Attack. Commit troops first. The defender rolls one die at a time.

3

Manoeuvre. One move. Rivers and mountains stop it.

4

Draw cards. 1 territory card per territory conquered.

5

End of turn. Roll: a 6 moves the cavalry. Move your pact cavalry on.

Centuries

Who starts: everyone rolls; highest goes first, re-roll ties. The first player opens the 5th century.

Opening: whoever's turn moves the golden cavalry opens the next century. They roll its event, mark it with the yellow cavalry, and stand one of their own cavalry on the century.

Moving on: after each turn, that player rolls: a 6 moves the cavalry. Otherwise it moves when play comes back round to the opener. Ignore the printed dice gates.

The end: the 14th ends at the end of a round, or on a 6. Reaching the 15th ends the game at once. Most territories wins.
