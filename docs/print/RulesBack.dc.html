Alliance pacts

Sign: two players agree 1–4 turns each and stake cards face up on a charter: 1 card each for 1–2 turns, 2 each for 3–4 turns. One pact per player.
During: the two may not attack each other.
Kept: when both have taken their turns, each takes back their stake plus (turns − 1) bonus cards, at most 3. Renewing straight away is fine.
Broken: the moment one attacks the other, the victim takes back their stake and the betrayer's is discarded. The betrayer draws cards as normal but is an oathbreaker: no pacts until the cavalry moves.
Tested: loyal players win 28%, well-timed betrayers 26%, constant backstabbers 21%, players who never sign 25%. Short pacts beat long ones.

Century events

Each time the cavalry lands on a century, including the 5th at the start, roll a die and lay that century's card in the Irish Sea beside the ages track: 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6. It lasts until the cavalry moves. "At once" effects happen immediately.
Leader: most territories right now (tied: earliest in turn order). Thinnest: fewest troops. Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops; no card drawn.
Tested: the deck raises comebacks from 23% to 34% of games and shrinks the winning margin by a quarter. Events lean against the leader and get harsher with time.
