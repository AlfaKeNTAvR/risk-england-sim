Alliance pacts

Two pacts exist, purple and orange. One pact per player.
Sign: agree 1, 2 or 3 turns each and take a treaty card. Each tucks territory cards face up under their side, worth exactly as many stars as turns, and stands one of their cavalry on their number of turns. Can't make the exact amount: you can't sign.
During: the two may not attack each other. After each of your turns, move your cavalry one space on.
Kept: both cavalry on “kept”. Each takes back their stake plus (turns − 1) cards, so at most 2. Renewing straight away is fine.
Broken: the moment one attacks the other, the victim takes both stakes. The betrayer draws cards as normal but takes an oathbreaker seal: no pacts until the next century.

Century events

When a century opens, including the 5th at the start, the opener rolls a die: 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6. They stand one of their own cavalry on that event on the plaque or era board, or lay its card beside the ages track. It lasts until the cavalry moves. "At once" effects happen immediately.
Leader: most territories right now (tied: earliest in turn order).
Thinnest: fewest troops (tied: the owner picks).
Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops. No card is drawn.
Losses never take a territory below 1 troop.
