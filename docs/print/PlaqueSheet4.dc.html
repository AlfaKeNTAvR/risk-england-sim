How the plaques work
  1. Stack the plaques in century order, 5th on top, beside the ages track.
  2. When the cavalry lands on a century, that plaque is on top. Roll a die and put a marker on the dashed circle of the rolled event: a spare piece or a coin.
  3. When the cavalry moves, move the plaque to the bottom of the stack.
  4. No century is skipped: a 6 only ends the current one early.
Words on the plaques
Leader: most territories right now. Tied: earliest in turn order.
Thinnest: fewest troops. Tied: the owner picks.
Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops. No card drawn.
Losses never take a territory below 1.
