Proportions follow your photos. Measure the sea areas on your own board before printing; sizes below assume a board about 20 by 26 inches.

Table pieces for the house rules

Everything fits in the sea around the map, so nothing new sits beside the board.
A
Active era card, in the Irish Sea
Right beside the printed ages track, where the golden cavalry stands. When the cavalry lands on a century, roll and lay that century's card here. The other 29 cards wait in a sorted stack off the board. 4 sheets, 30 cards at 2.4 x 3.25 in.
B C
Purple and orange pacts
Two pact colours, chosen so they never match an army. A treaty card sits at the edge of the board between the two signers, with each stake tucked under its side. The square seals are a smaller alternative.
D
Spare spot in the English Channel
Between the Scilly and Carisbrooke boxes. Use it for oathbreaker seals, or for the era card if the Irish Sea feels crowded.
Printing
Letter paper, print at 100% (no fit-to-page). Laminate the whole sheet, then cut each piece along its outline with a craft knife and round the corners with the 5 mm punch.
Every piece has a 4 mm gap to its neighbours and 5 mm of clear space inside its edge, so the punch never touches text.
