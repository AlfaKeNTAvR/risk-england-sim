Pacts in brief
Sign: agree 1–4 turns each. Stake 1 card each for 1–2 turns, 2 cards each for 3–4 turns. One pact per player.
Kept: stakes back, plus (turns − 1) bonus cards each, at most 3.
Broken: the victim takes back their stake; the betrayer's is discarded and they take an oathbreaker seal.
