Defending one die at a time

Before a battle, move the attacking troops across, leaving at least 1 behind. The attacker rolls first. The defender rolls one die, looks, and with 2 or more troops may roll a second. Ties go to the defender.

Winners occupy the territory; no extra troops move in until your manoeuvre.

Rivers and mountains

Manoeuvre: crossing a river, at most 5 troops, and they stop on the far side. Entering a mountain territory, they stop there.

Attack: into a mountain or across a river, remove 1 committed troop before the first roll, once per battle. Leeds to Beeston costs 2.

Rivers: Leeds–Beeston, Leeds–Bolsover, Leeds–Conisbrough, Portchester–Donnington, Donnington–Farnham, Farnham–Jewel Tower, Jewel Tower–Dover, Colchester–Dover.

Mountains: Carlisle, Brougham, Cliffords, Beeston, Wall, St Briavels, Old Sarum.

Rulings

Power card losses hit only the player who flips them; Heavy snowfall hits only your North territories. No territory drops below 1. For “lose 2” or “lose 3”, use a territory that can take the full loss if you have one, else your largest.

Jewel Tower mission: 3 turns in total, anyone's turns count.
