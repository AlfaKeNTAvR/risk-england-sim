The Ages of England The golden cavalry stands on its century. The opener rolls and stands one of their own
cavalry on the rolled event. Turn over after the IX.
V 410–499
golden
1–2 Saxon migration
Whole century Every player gets 2 extra troops at the start of each turn.
3–4 Roman roads
Whole century You may make 2 manoeuvres per turn. Rivers and mountains don’t stop them.
5–6 Land grab
At once Every player except the leader draws 1 territory card.
VI 500–599
golden
1–2 Petty kingdoms
At once Every player except the leader gets 2 extra troops on their next turn.
3–4 Plague of Justinian
At once Every territory with 3 or more troops loses 1.
5–6 Age of saints
Whole century Mission bonuses are doubled.
VII 600–699
golden
1–2 Shield walls
Whole century Attackers roll at most 2 dice.
3–4 Peace of the Church
Whole century Pacts can’t be broken. Pacts signed this century pay 1 extra bonus card.
5–6 Underdog kings
Whole century At the start of each turn, the player with the fewest territories gets 3 extra troops.
VIII 700–799
golden
1–2 Viking raids
At once + century The leader’s port territories each lose 2 troops. No attacks along sea routes this century.
3–4 Offa’s Dyke
Whole century Every border between two regions counts as a river crossing for manoeuvres.
5–6 Good harvests
Whole century At the start of each turn, get 1 extra troop for every region where you hold 3 or more territories.
IX 800–899
golden
1–2 Great Heathen Army
Whole century When attacking the leader, add 1 to your highest die.
3–4 Danegeld
Whole century Before trading on their turn, the leader gives their lowest-star card to the player with the fewest territories. With no cards, they place 2 fewer troops and that player gets 2.
5–6 Alfred’s burhs
Whole century Defenders add 1 to their highest die, except the leader.
The Ages of England, continued The XIV ends at the end of a round, or on a 6.
XV: 1400–1485. The game ends at once. Most territories wins.
X 900–999
golden
1–2 One kingdom
Whole century At the start of each turn, every player except the leader gets 3 extra troops.
3–4 Battle of Brunanburh
Whole century Grand alliance: players other than the leader may only attack the leader.
5–6 Hard winter
At once + century Every territory with 6 or more troops loses 2. No manoeuvres into mountain territories this century.
XI 1000–1099
golden
1–2 Norman castles
Whole century Defending a territory with 5 or more troops, the defender may roll 3 dice.
3–4 Harrying of the North
At once The leader’s North territories lose half their troops, rounded down.
5–6 Domesday survey
Whole century The leader draws at most 1 territory card per turn, however many territories they conquer.
XII 1100–1199
golden
1–2 The Anarchy
At once The leader’s 2 thinnest territories defect to the player with the fewest territories.
3–4 Great floods
Whole century No manoeuvres across rivers. Attacks across a river crossing roll at most 1 die.
5–6 Supply problems
Whole century At the start of the leader’s turn, each of their territories with 5 or more troops loses a third, rounded down.
XIII 1200–1299
golden
1–2 Magna Carta
Whole century The leader can’t trade cards this century.
3–4 Barons’ War
At once + century All pacts end: stakes go back, no bonus cards. No new pacts this century.
5–6 Camp fever
At once The leader’s largest stack loses half its troops, rounded down.
XIV 1300–1399
golden
1–2 Black Death
At once Every territory loses half its troops, rounded down, and every hand of territory cards is halved. The player with the fewest territories is spared.
3–4 Great Famine
Final count At the final count, territories with only 1 troop count as half.
5–6 Peasants’ Revolt
At once The leader’s 3 thinnest territories defect to the player with the fewest territories.
