Risk: The Battle for Medieval England

Our House Rules

Everything not here follows the rulebook.

Your turn

1

Place troops. 3, plus the territory chart, regions, missions and card trades. You may flip a power card.

2

Attack. Commit troops first. The defender rolls one die at a time.

3

Manoeuvre. One move. Rivers and mountains stop it.

4

Draw cards. 1 territory card per territory conquered.

5

End of turn. Roll: a 6 moves the cavalry. Move your pact cavalry on.

Centuries

Who starts: everyone rolls; highest goes first, re-roll ties. The first player opens the 5th century.

Opening: whoever's turn moves the golden cavalry opens the next century. They roll its event and stand one of their own cavalry on it: that marks the active event and who opened it.

Moving on: after each turn, that player rolls: a 6 moves the cavalry. Otherwise it moves when play comes back round to the opener. Ignore the printed dice gates.

The end: the 14th ends at the end of a round, or on a 6. Reaching the 15th ends the game at once. Most territories wins.

House rules · 1

Defending one die at a time

Before a battle, move the attacking troops across, leaving at least 1 behind. The attacker rolls first. The defender rolls one die, looks, and with 2 or more troops may roll a second. Ties go to the defender.

Winners occupy the territory; no extra troops move in until your manoeuvre.

Rivers and mountains

Manoeuvre: crossing a river, at most 5 troops, and they stop on the far side. Entering a mountain territory, they stop there.

Attack: into a mountain or across a river, remove 1 committed troop before the first roll, once per battle. Leeds to Beeston costs 2.

Rivers: Leeds–Beeston, Leeds–Bolsover, Leeds–Conisbrough, Portchester–Donnington, Donnington–Farnham, Farnham–Jewel Tower, Jewel Tower–Dover, Colchester–Dover.

Mountains: Carlisle, Brougham, Cliffords, Beeston, Wall, St Briavels, Old Sarum.

Rulings

Power card losses hit only the player who flips them; Heavy snowfall hits only your North territories. No territory drops below 1. For “lose 2” or “lose 3”, use a territory that can take the full loss if you have one, else your largest.

Jewel Tower mission: 3 turns in total, anyone's turns count.

House rules · 2

Alliance pacts

Two pacts exist, purple and orange. One pact per player.

Sign: agree 1, 2 or 3 turns each and take a treaty card. Each tucks territory cards face up under their side, worth exactly as many stars as turns, and stands one of their cavalry on their number of turns. Can't make the exact amount: you can't sign.

During: the two may not attack each other. After each of your turns, move your cavalry one space on.

Kept: both cavalry on “kept”. Each takes back their stake plus (turns − 1) cards, so at most 2. Renewing straight away is fine.

Broken: the moment one attacks the other, the victim takes both stakes. The betrayer draws cards as normal but takes an oathbreaker seal: no pacts until the next century.

Century events

When a century opens, including the 5th at the start, the opener rolls a die: 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6. They stand one of their own cavalry on that event on the plaque or era board, or lay its card beside the ages track. It lasts until the cavalry moves. “At once” effects happen immediately.

Leader: most territories right now (tied: earliest in turn order).

Thinnest: fewest troops (tied: the owner picks).

Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops. No card is drawn.

Losses never take a territory below 1 troop.
