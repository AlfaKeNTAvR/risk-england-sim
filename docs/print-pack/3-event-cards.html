How century events work
  1. Keep the cards in a stack sorted by century, off the board.
  2. When a century opens, including the 5th at the start, the opener rolls a die and takes that century's card for the roll.
  3. Lay it face up in the Irish Sea beside the ages track. It stays active until the cavalry moves again.
  4. "At once" effects happen immediately. The 15th century has no card: reaching it ends the game.
Words on the cards
Leader: most territories right now. Tied: earliest in turn order.
Thinnest: fewest troops. Tied: the owner picks.
Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops. No card is drawn.
Losses never take a territory below 1 troop.
