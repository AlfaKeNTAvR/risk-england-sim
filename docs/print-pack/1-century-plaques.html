How the plaques work
  1. Stack the plaques beside the board, V face up on top.
  2. Whoever's turn moves the cavalry opens the next century: they roll a die and stand one of their own cavalry on the rolled event. It shows both the active event and who opened the century. The first player opens the V.
  3. When the cavalry moves on, flip the plaque. After an even century, move it to the bottom of the stack.
  4. No century is skipped: a 6 only ends the current one early.
Words on the plaques
Leader: most territories right now. Tied: earliest in turn order.
Thinnest: fewest troops. Tied: the owner picks.
Defect: the territory goes to the player with the fewest territories, with 1 of their troops. No card drawn.
Losses never take a territory below 1.

XIV century 1300–1399
1–2 Black Death
At once
Every territory loses half its troops, rounded down, and every hand of territory cards is halved. The player with the fewest territories is spared.
The plague kills a third of England from 1348.
3–4 Great Famine
Final count
At the final count, territories with only 1 troop count as half.
Years of failed harvests from 1315 empty the thinly held lands.
5–6 Peasants’ Revolt
At once
The leader’s 3 thinnest territories defect to the player with the fewest territories.
Rebels march on London in 1381.
