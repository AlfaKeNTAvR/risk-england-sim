Ages track

Official rule: the cavalry moves 1 space after every player's turn. At a dice gate (red), that player must roll the printed even or odd number, or it stays put.

Troops for cards

Deck: 18 one-star and 24 two-star cards, 66 stars in total. At setup, each dealt card puts 1 infantry per star on its territory.

Troops for territories

Every turn: 3 infantry, plus 12-14 territories: +1, then +1 for every 3 more, up to 40-42: +10. One card drawn per territory conquered. Pieces: infantry 1, cavalry 5, artillery 10.

Mission cards

Each player is dealt 2. Tap one to highlight its territories on the map. Bonus troops arrive at the start of your next turn.

    Power cards

    Each player gets 2, face down. You may flip one during Place Your Troops. Loss cards apply at once; keepable cards can be saved for any moment, including combat.

    House rules: loss cards only hit the player who flips them. A territory never drops below 1 troop. For a fixed loss (lose 2 or 3), pick a territory that can take the full loss if you have one; otherwise take it from your largest territory, down to 1.
